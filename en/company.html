Company

KOREA VISION ENERTECH is a fuel technology company. Since completing our blend database in 1998, we have researched and tested high-alcohol blended fuel.

About us

We do not just add a little alcohol to gasoline. We rebuild the fuel from 50% alcohol, petrochemical raw materials and additive. Outside bodies have tested it, including Pusan National University, the Shanghai Chemical Industry Research Institute, Sinopec Fushun Research Institute and the Korea Petroleum Quality & Distribution Authority.

Our core technology is the blending method and the additive manufacturing method. We hold patents in Korea and China, have filed internationally, and have registered trademarks.

How the fuel is made

Seven steps from delivery of raw materials to dispatch.

  1. Raw material receivingCheck water content and quality of incoming material
  2. Unloading and transferKeep water out; use pipes of suitable material
  3. Raw material storageStore so moisture is not absorbed
  4. Blending and mixingMix evenly in a set order
  5. Quality inspectionCheck octane number and phase stability
  6. Finished product storageControl water; keep storage stable
  7. Product dispatchCheck quality before dispatch

This diagram only explains the flow. Detailed control standards for each step are not disclosed.
