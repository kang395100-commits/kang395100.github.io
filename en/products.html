What the additive does

Ingredient names and mix ratios are trade secrets, so we describe only what each part does.

  • Stops phase separation

    Keeps the fuel from splitting into layers in the cold, in long storage, or when water gets in.

  • Stops metal corrosion

    Protects metal parts.

  • Antioxidant

    Slows down the fuel from going stale.

  • Surfactant and compatibilizer

    Helps the alcohol and petroleum parts mix evenly.

The petrochemical raw material is a common chemical mix, not a patented material. Our core patented technology is the blending method and the additive manufacturing method.

Frequently asked questions

The questions people ask most when they consider our fuel.

Do engines or cars need to be changed?
No. It is designed for today's gasoline engines and today's fuel supply and sales network.
Won't the fuel split into layers in the cold or in storage?
MESOLEAN showed no phase separation at −24℃ for more than 365 days.
Will metal and rubber parts corrode?
In corrosion tests on metals (copper, aluminum, steel) and rubber (NBR, fluoro rubber), results matched gasoline (MESOLEAN result).
How is it different from other alcohol blends?
We do not just add a little alcohol to gasoline. We rebuild the fuel from 50% alcohol, petrochemical raw materials and additive.
