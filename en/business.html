Partnership

Two ways to cooperate, to fit your situation: a technology license or a joint venture.

Work with us

If you already have production and supply networks, a license fits. If you want to enter a local market together, a joint venture fits.

Technology license

The partner uses our technology to make and sell the fuel itself

What we provide

  • Right to use our patented technology (blending and additive methods)
  • Product quality standards and test methods
  • Technical training and start-up support

What the partner does

  • Prepare production, storage and delivery facilities
  • Buy raw materials and get local permits
  • Sales and customer care

Good for refiners and fuel sellers that already have production and supply networks

Joint venture

We set up a joint venture with the partner to make and sell locally

What we do

  • Provide the technology and take part in quality control
  • Process design and testing support
  • Take part in running the joint venture

What the partner does

  • Provide capital and land
  • Local permits and sales channels
  • Raw material supply and local operations

Good for investors and energy companies that want to enter a local market together

How we start

An example. The exact scope and terms are decided together with each partner.

  1. Contact usTell us by email what you are interested in and where.
  2. Technical briefing and data reviewWe explain the technology and share test data.
  3. Discuss termsWe agree on the way of working together and its scope.
  4. Agreement and startAfter signing, we support the start-up.

Current project

In May 2020, Botian Petroleum of Taiyuan, Shanxi and KOREA VISION ENERTECH set up the joint venture Shanxi Zhongheng New Energy Technology Development Co., Ltd. In March 2023, the enterprise investment project was registered in Shanxi Province.

The planned production and supply site has finished product tanks, a loading area, offices and support buildings.
