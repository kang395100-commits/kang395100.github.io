Why alcohol blended fuel, and why now?

The move to electric and hydrogen cars happens step by step. Until then, today's gasoline cars keep running.

  • Change takes time

    Moving to renewable energy, electric cars and hydrogen happens step by step. Until then, today's gasoline cars keep running.

  • Cutting greenhouse gases cannot wait

    Under the 2015 Paris Agreement, carbon-based industries must cut greenhouse gas emissions step by step.

  • Many places lack charging and hydrogen stations

    We need a fix that works with today's cars and gas stations, without large new networks.

  • More choice of energy sources

    Methanol and ethanol can lower our need for crude oil and support energy security.
